Who we are

Electrical Expertise You Can Rely On

Pure Power Electrical Engineering Service is a Ghanaian electrical engineering company established in 2018, providing electrical solutions for residential, commercial, and industrial clients.

We approach every project with a focus on proper planning, professional workmanship, safety, and dependable results. From electrical installation and maintenance to security and automation systems, our goal is to deliver solutions that work properly and continue to perform.
