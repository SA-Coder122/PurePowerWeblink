Terms of Use

This website is provided by Pure Power Electrical Engineering Service to share information about electrical services and to receive enquiries. By using the site, you agree to use it lawfully and not to misuse forms, contact details or published content.

Content on the website is for general information. It does not replace a site visit, a written quotation, or a contract for electrical work.

Service Terms

Service descriptions on this website are general. The actual scope of work depends on project assessment, site conditions, available materials and the instructions you provide.

Quotes may change when project requirements change, when hidden conditions are found, or when the requested specification is revised.

Quotes & Pricing

A quote may depend on materials, labour, site conditions and project scope. A quote should not be treated as binding until it has been accepted under Pure Power’s confirmed process.

Published pages do not list standard prices because electrical work varies by property and specification.

Payments

Payment methods, deposits and due dates are confirmed directly by Pure Power for each project. This website does not collect card payments and does not publish deposit percentages or payment deadlines that have not been supplied by the business.

Cancellations

Cancellation conditions depend on the status of the job, materials already purchased and any written agreement. Ask Pure Power to confirm cancellation terms before work begins.

Warranties

This website does not state product or workmanship warranties beyond what Pure Power confirms for a specific project. Any warranty that applies will be described in the company’s own documentation or quotation.

Website Disclaimer

The website is not a substitute for a professional site assessment where one is required. Photographs used on the site may include representative industry imagery until approved project photographs are provided by Pure Power.

Privacy Policy

When you send an enquiry, we may collect your name, phone number, email address, message, selected service and project location. This information is used to review the request, respond to you and keep a record of the enquiry.

We do not sell personal information. Contact details submitted through the form are processed so that Pure Power can reply. Form submissions may also pass through the form delivery provider used to receive messages by email.

To ask a question about personal information sent through this website, contact info@purepowerelectricalservice.com or call 024 073 6896.

Cookies

This website is built as a static marketing site. It does not currently set analytics or advertising cookies of its own. Your browser may still store essential data needed to display pages.

If you use the Google Maps embed on the Contact page, Google may set cookies or similar technologies according to Google’s own policies. If analytics or advertising tags are added later, this section will be updated before those tools are used.
